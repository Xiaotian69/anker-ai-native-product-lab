# AI Council prompt — recorded input

Review the five normalized opportunity candidates as independent roles: user researcher, competitor analyst, product manager, business traveler, hardware engineer, supply-chain specialist, compliance specialist, and red-team reviewer.

For each role:

1. identify the strongest claim it accepts;
2. challenge at least one unsupported assumption;
3. link the challenge to evidence IDs when available;
4. state a concrete product consequence;
5. label the output `RECORDED_AI_REVIEW`, never “expert opinion” or “user research.”

The goal is to expose conflicts and validation work, not to reach unanimous approval.
